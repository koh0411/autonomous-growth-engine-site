Privacy Policy

Project scope

Autonomous Growth Engine is currently a personal-use project. It assists the owner with content operations for the owner’s own connected accounts.

Data accessed

When the owner authorizes a platform connection, the project may access data through the platform’s authorized APIs. Depending on the permissions granted, this data may include account information, boards, Pins or other content, and analytics or performance data.

How data is used

Authorized data is used to publish content that the owner has approved, measure content performance, and improve later content decisions. Production publishing requires explicit human approval.

Data practices

  • Data is not sold.
  • Credentials and access tokens are not intentionally exposed publicly.
  • Access is limited to permissions granted through the relevant platform and API.
  • The project does not claim access beyond what the connected platform authorizes.

Revoking access

Where supported, the owner can revoke the project’s authorization through the settings or authorization controls provided by the relevant platform.

Independent project

This project is not an official service of, endorsed by, or affiliated with Pinterest or any other connected platform.

Policy changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the project changes. The effective date above will be revised when the published policy is updated.
